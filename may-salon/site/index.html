About

Hair that looks good after you go home

The test for a good cut is how it looks after you blow-dry it yourself, not how it looks in the salon chair. That is the standard we work to.

The Korean owner designs every Korean cut, perm and color herself, from consultation to the final blow-dry. Our Filipino stylists handle nails, eyelash extensions and Filipino-style hair services like rebond and Brazilian.

“Lighter hair, lighter days.”
